### General Review

My mentor guided me patiently, explained concepts clearly, and encouraged independent problem-solving before stepping in. The admin team handled paperwork efficiently and ensured I had the resources needed to perform well.

**3. Academic Relevance**  
The assignments matched my university background while also exposing me to new areas. This balance allowed me to reinforce my knowledge and gain practical insights.

**4. Learning Opportunities**  
I improved my skills in project management tools, teamwork, and professional communication. My mentor’s real-world advice was especially valuable for shaping my career direction.

**5. Company Culture**  
The culture was respectful and collaborative. Everyone contributed during urgent projects regardless of role, which made me feel truly integrated into the team.

**6. Internship Benefits**  
The internship allowance, flexible hours, and access to internal training sessions were meaningful benefits that enhanced the experience.
